# Metric review — balance sheet & cash flow group

Reviewed against: Sloan 1996 (accruals), Novy-Marx 2013 (gross
profitability).

Group context: this panel's research weight comes from **Sloan's accruals
split**: earnings = cash flow (persistence 0.855) + accruals (persistence
0.765, reverting within 1–3 years). The market prices both at ~0.9, so
high-accrual firms are overpriced: the lowest-minus-highest accrual decile
hedge earned **10.4% in year t+1 (t = 4.71), positive in 28 of 30 years**,
with 40% of the payoff clustering around the next four earnings
announcements. The signature is current assets (receivables, inventory)
growing without matching liabilities. Longfund cannot compute exact
balance-sheet accruals (no ΔCA/ΔCL history from Yahoo `info`), so this panel
serves as the qualitative earnings-quality read: NI vs OCF, current ratio.

### `netIncomeToCommon` — net income
- what: trailing 12-month profit to common.
- research: one leg of the accrual read — watch it *against* operating cash

  accruals (persistence 0.855 vs 0.765).
- reference: n/a (scale)
- hover: "Trailing 12-month net income to common — watch it against operating cash flow: earnings split into persistent cash flow and fast-reverting accruals"

### `operatingCashflow` — op. cash flow
- what: cash generated by operations.
- research: **the persistent leg of the Sloan split** — the component of
  earnings that survives. When NI ≫ OCF, earnings are accrual-heavy and
  prone to revert.
- reference: n/a (scale); read as NI ÷ OCF ≈ 1 is healthy, NI ≫ OCF is the
  accrual red flag
- hover: "Cash generated by operations — the persistent leg of the earnings-quality split: cash-backed earnings persist (persistence ≈0.86), accrual-backed earnings mostly vanish within 1–3 years"

### `freeCashflow` — free cash flow
- what: OCF − capex.
- research: the cash the business frees up; capex discipline context for
  the OCF read.
- reference: n/a (scale)
- hover: "Operating cash flow − capex; cash the business frees up"

### `totalCash` / `totalDebt` / `netDebt`
- what: cash pile, debt stack, difference.
- research: leverage context — the amplifier on every other number; also the
  survive-a-drawdown question for long-horizon holders.
- reference: n/a (scale); net debt ≈ 0 or negative = fortress
- hover (totalCash): "Cash and short-term investments on the balance sheet"
- hover (totalDebt): "Short- plus long-term debt — the leverage that amplifies everything else on this page"
- hover (netDebt): "Total debt − total cash; what is owed beyond the cash pile"

### `currentRatio` — current ratio
- what: current assets ÷ current liabilities.
- research: the observable proxy for the accrual signature — when
  receivables and inventory grow without matching liabilities, earnings are
  accrual-heavy (that is exactly the Sloan current-asset component).
- reference: ≈ 1.6 median; drifting up on the asset side without liability
  growth is the red flag
- hover: "Current assets ÷ current liabilities. When receivables and inventory grow without matching liabilities, earnings are accrual-heavy — the earnings-quality anomaly's signature (the accrual hedge returned +10.4% in its first year, positive 28 of 30 years)"

### `debtToEquity` — debt / equity
- what: total debt ÷ equity.
- research: leverage context; note the research deliberately avoids
  equity-scaled measures (GP/A scales by assets to stay
  leverage-independent).
- reference: ≈ 0.8× median
- hover: "Total debt ÷ shareholder equity — leverage context"

## Candidate add (not in product): accruals

Exact Sloan accruals need two consecutive balance sheets (ΔCA, ΔCash, ΔCL,
ΔSTD, ΔTP, Dep) — not available from Yahoo `info`. The NI-vs-OCF pair plus
current-ratio drift is the honest proxy available today.
